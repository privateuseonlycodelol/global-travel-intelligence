## Ranking rule

1. City population ≥ 1,000,000 (GeoNames).
2. One principal city per GHSL urban centre: the largest GeoNames city in the
   centre, not the highest-scoring suburb.
3. GHSL centre population ≥ 25% of city population. This drops prefecture or
   county records whose point is not an urban area.
4. Rank by the score; tie-break by city population.

Counts: 551 million-plus cities → 478 with a park-inclusive greenery score →
417 principal cities → 407 after the boundary filter.

## GHSL-native rankings

`ghsl_physical_greenery_top100` and `ghsl_environment_top100` use all GHSL
centres ≥1M with a GeoNames label (516 of 524). Physical greenery is measured
inside the GHSL polygon at native 10 m with cos(latitude) weighting; 497 pass
the 20% built-up filter. `ghsl_scores_all_516.csv` includes the raw fractions
and missing flags. Nature access and everyday greenery are blocked; see
`docs/GHSL_METHOD_AUDIT.md`.

The earlier `urban_greenery_top100` and `surrounding_nature_top100` files are a
historical 482-centre pass and are not the current GHSL-native rankings.

```
urban_green = 100 * (tree + shrub + grass) / (1 - water)   # inside polygon
urban_tree  = 100 * tree / (1 - water)                     # inside polygon
annulus_F   = (225 * F_15km - F_1km) / 224
surrounding = 0.80 * 100 * (annulus_tree + annulus_shrub) / (1 - annulus_water)
            + 0.20 * water_access_score
```

## Formulas

```
greenery = 0.25 * local_tree_score
         + 0.15 * local_other_veg_score
         + 0.45 * hinterland_tree_shrub_score
         + 0.15 * park_score

environment = 0.50 * greenery
            + 0.35 * air_score
            + 0.15 * water_score
```

`local_*` and `hinterland_*` are land-only shares: water pixels are removed
from the denominator. `park_score = min(100, 100 * ln(1 + park_count) /
ln(501))`. `air_score` is a fixed log scale from 100 at 5 µg/m³ to 0 at
75 µg/m³. `water_score` is `100 * max(0, 1 - d/50 km)`, where `d` is the
nearest coast, lake ≥10 km² or Strahler ≥4 river.

## Column notes

- `local_tree_pct`, `local_shrub_pct`, `local_grass_pct`: 1 km land-only
  percentages.
- `hinterland_tree_pct`, `hinterland_shrub_pct`: 15 km land-only percentages.
- `local_water_pct`, `hinterland_water_pct`: water share of all pixels at each
  radius; removed from the vegetation denominator.
- `centre_population`: GHSL urban-centre population, shown for boundary
  context, not used in the score.
- `water_feature`: `river`, `large_lake` or `coast`; `water_distance_km` is
  the minimum of the three.
- `combined_score_0_100`: 50% greenery + 50% environment.
- `pm25_ugm3`: annual 2024 SatPM estimate; `worst_month_pm25` is the monthly
  maximum.
- A zero park count is `none_listed` and is excluded, not scored as zero.
