# Combined physical greenery + environmental quality

**Audit date:** 2026-11-25

This is the literal 50/50 blend of the two published scores:

```
combined = 0.50 × physical greenery
         + 0.50 × environmental quality
```

Because environmental quality already contains physical greenery, the
effective component weights are:

```
combined = 0.75 × greenery
         + 0.175 × air
         + 0.075 × water access
```

That is deliberate: it is the answer to “combine the two lists”, not an
equal-weight component index. If you want the 50% greenery / 35% air / 15%
water balance, use the environmental-quality score instead.

The ranking unit is the same as the other two: city population ≥1M, one
principal city per GHSL urban centre, 25% centre/city boundary filter. 407
cities are ranked.
